### **Question 1 — Rule 1: Precedence Hierarchy**
#### **a) Placement of `dex_dave` (Tier 3, weight 0.90) vs. Product Collections (Tier 2, 0.80–0.95)**
**Position:** **LOCK**
- **Rationale:** `dex_dave` (operator voice) should outrank product collections for intent/voice queries but not for domain-specific governance. The tier/weight distinction is intentional:
  - **Tier 3** ensures `dex_dave` is excluded from governance queries (Rule 3).
  - **Weight 0.90** ensures it scores higher than product collections (0.80–0.95) for non-governance queries.
  - **Edge Case:** For schema-specific queries (e.g., "AuditForge schema"), the product collection (e.g., `dex_auditforge`) will win due to Rule 4’s compound routing (GOVERNANCE + PRODUCT).

#### **b) `dex_lore` (0.95, Tier 2) vs. `dex_dave` (0.90, Tier 3)**
**Position:** **LOCK**
- **Rationale:** The conflict is intentional. Tier gates eligibility; weight governs scoring within eligible collections. For lore-adjacent queries, `dex_lore` (0.95) will outscore `dex_dave` (0.90). For operator-voice queries, `dex_dave` is the only eligible collection (Rule 4).

#### **c) `dex_decisions` in Tier 5 (weight 0.95) before it exists**
**Position:** **LOCK**
- **Rationale:** Pre-emptive authority is justified because:
  - It will contain ratified decisions (e.g., this ADR).
  - `dex_canon` (1.00) already exists and will outrank it.
  - Risk is mitigated by Rule 3 (governance isolation).

#### **d) Missing/Incorrectly Placed Collections**
**Position:** **REVISE**
- **Issue:** `dex_graveyard` (Tier 0) is not in the default retrieval path, but its weight (0.80) suggests it could be consulted for anti-patterns. Clarify:
  - **Proposed Fix:** Add a note that `dex_graveyard` is **never** included in default retrieval, even for anti-pattern queries. Use only via explicit `--collection dex_graveyard` flag.
