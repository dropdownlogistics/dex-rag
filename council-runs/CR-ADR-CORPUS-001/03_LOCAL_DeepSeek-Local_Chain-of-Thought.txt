#### **Question 1 — RULE 1: PRECEDENCE HIERARCHY**  
**a) Placement of dex_dave vs. Product Collections**  
**Verdict:** REVISE  
**Reasoning:**  
The placement of `dex_dave` (Tier 4, weight 0.90) above Tier 2 product collections (weight 0.80–0.95) creates ambiguity in retrieval scoring. While `dex_dave` carries operator intent authority, product collections (e.g., `dex_auditforge`) are governed artifacts. Queries about product-specific domains (e.g., "AuditForge schema") should prioritize the governed artifact (`dex_auditforge`) over operator voice (`dex_dave`), even if `dex_dave` has higher tier eligibility. This misalignment risks operator bias overriding formal standards.  

**b) Weight/Tier Conflict (dex_dave vs. dex_lore)**  
**Verdict:** LOCK  
**Reasoning:**  
The conflict is intentional and resolved correctly by tier gates eligibility and weight-based scoring. `dex_lore` (Tier 3, weight 0.95) outranks `dex_dave` (Tier 4, weight 0.90) only when both are eligible (e.g., non-governance queries). Tier eligibility trumps weight, so `dex_lore` is scored higher for lore-adjacent content. The router must enforce this by excluding `dex_dave` from governance queries (Rule 3).  

**c) dex_decisions Placement**  
**Verdict:** REVISE  
**Reasoning:**  
`dex_decisions` (Tier 5, weight 0.95) is not yet built but is assigned governance authority. This creates a governance paradox: a non-existent collection governs retrieval precedence. Ratified standards (`dex_canon`) should not be overridden by non-built collections. Move `dex_decisions` to Tier 0 (meta) or delay its precedence until live.  

**d) Missing/Incorrect Collections**

**Reasoning:**
